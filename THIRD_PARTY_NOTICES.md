## QuantumGraph and pairwise-tomography

The offline compiler uses pinned public revisions of Moth's QuantumGraph and pairwise-tomography packages through the sibling `quantumgraph-playground` Python environment:

- QuantumGraph commit `6917364b9496bd324225e87e6dd986bce52ecefd`
- pairwise-tomography commit `dbab12513281bd8ca7828252cf2e98a1a5749761`

The prototype does not redistribute or modify their source in this directory. The compiler directly preserves the pair fitter's `hi tensor lo` ordering and performs explicit transpile/run acquisition so job IDs, circuit hashes, seeds, shot totals, and raw-count digests are retained.

## Qiskit and Qiskit Aer

The offline compiler uses Qiskit 2.2.3 and Qiskit Aer 0.17.2 in the pinned sibling Python environment. The committed bank was generated by a local finite-shot Aer simulator. Browser play does not bundle or execute Qiskit or Aer.

Project: <https://www.ibm.com/quantum/qiskit>
Licenses: Apache License 2.0 in the respective distributions.

## Runtime art and legacy sources

The browser loads generated PNG sheets for six chickens, two commentator portraits, and one shield icon. They are deterministic outputs of the checked-in, hand-specified pixel definitions in `scripts/generate-pixel-assets.mjs`. Earlier SVG chicken and commentator sources remain under `art-source/legacy-svg/`; they are not copied into the production build. No external font, stock illustration, linked raster, or image-generation service is used. See `art-source/ART_PROVENANCE.md` and `LICENSES.md`.
